# Vegetarian Chick’n Pot Pie with Cheddar Biscuit Topping

## Ben Chaney

A tasty savory treat

**Serves:** 6 
**Prep Time:** 20 min 
**Cook Time:** 55 min

## Ingredients

- 1 small yellow onion
- 1 cup+ any: potatoes, celery, carrots, mushrooms, peas, white beans, kale, sausage crumbles
- ¼ cups fresh parsley, sage, thyme leaves
- 4 Tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
- ¼ cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 1 can biscuits


## Instructions

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 400°F.

2. Dice all veggies

3. Melt 4 tablespoons unsalted butter in a large cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chopped vegetables, 4 thyme sprigs, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper. Cook until the vegetables begin to soften, about 8 minutes.

4. Add 1/4 cup all-purpose flour, stir to coat the vegetables, and cook for about 30 seconds. Whisk 2 cups vegetable broth. Reduce the heat to maintain a simmer and cook until thick enough to coat the back of a spoon, 3 to 4 minutes. Stir in the sausage, parsley, and frozen peas. Remove from the heat.

5. Bake until the sauce is bubbling, ~15 min. If it needs thickening, add cornstarch slurry.

6. Tear biscuits in half & top veggies. Bake ~15 min, until tops are golden.

## Serving Suggestions
Eat with a spoon
